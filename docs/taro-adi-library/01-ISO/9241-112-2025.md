## Original operational rules

1. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H01** — Put the most task-relevant information where it is likely to be noticed at the decision point.
2. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H02** — Distinguish controls, static labels, status and error messages using role, location and structure.
3. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H03** — Keep copy short but preserve the information necessary to act safely and correctly.
4. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H04** — Use consistent units, date conventions and numeric precision within a screen.
5. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H05** — Group related information and align it to facilitate comparison.
6. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H06** — Suppress low-value ornaments and repetitive callouts that compete with the main task.
7. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H07** — Pair color encoding with labels or patterns when information must be understood reliably.
8. **ADI-ISO-9241_112_2025-H08** — Ensure charts, tables and nonvisual alerts retain meaning under high zoom or assistive access.

## What would fail the companion review

- Critical status tucked below the fold without indication
- Multiple unrelated numerics shown without units
- Three equivalent states with different iconography
- Animations distract from an error requiring attention
